Nonlinear waves in curved spacetimes

2025 — present

Topological phases in aperiodic systems

Developing a K-theoretic framework for classifying topological invariants of quasicrystalline quantum systems. The central object is the noncommutative Brillouin zone, which replaces the torus in the periodic setting. We establish a bulk-boundary correspondence via the index map

connecting the K-theory of the bulk algebra to edge-state invariants.

Completed projects
2022 — 2024

Spectral theory of random Schrödinger operators

Proved localization results for a class of random operators on metric graphs, extending Anderson localization to non-Euclidean geometries. Showed that for almost every realization of the random potential, the spectrum is pure point with exponentially decaying eigenfunctions.

2020 — 2022 · PhD Thesis

Geometric quantization of integrable systems

Developed geometric quantization techniques for integrable Hamiltonian systems with non-trivial monodromy, establishing connections between semiclassical analysis, symplectic topology, and quantum spectra. The main result shows that the quantum monodromy operator determines the joint spectrum modulo corrections.

Research interests (summary)

My research lies at the intersection of analysis, geometry, and mathematical physics. I am broadly interested in spectral theory, partial differential equations on manifolds, topological aspects of quantum mechanics, and the rigorous mathematical foundations of quantum field theory. I enjoy problems where tools from different areas of mathematics interact to illuminate physical phenomena.
