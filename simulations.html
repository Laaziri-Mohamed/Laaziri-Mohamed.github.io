Fortran · VEM · poromechanics

VEM fracture thermo-poro-mechanics solver

Open research code for 3D fracture poromechanics on polyhedral meshes. The solver couples pressure and temperature with mechanics and supports contact through Nitsche or mixed Lagrange-multiplier formulations with Coulomb friction.

CEA IRESNE · CIMAC · CAST3M

Multiphysics coupling for corium-concrete interaction

Postdoctoral development of numerical exchanges between a multiphase thermal-hydraulics code and a finite-element thermomechanics code. The workflow targets heat flux exchange, ablation-driven geometry updates, source terms, and thermomechanical response.
