thm-vem-fracture-contact-nitsche-lagrange

Fortran solver for 3D fracture poromechanics on polyhedral meshes. It combines VEM mechanics, Darcy/Fourier pressure-temperature coupling, Nitsche or mixed Lagrange multiplier contact, bubble DOFs, Coulomb friction, and visualization outputs.

How the code is organised conceptually

Geometry and mesh preprocessing

Cells, faces, fracture faces, barycentres, normal/tangential frames, jump operators, and degree-of-freedom mappings.

Mechanics and contact solve

VEM elasticity with either Nitsche contact or mixed Lagrange multipliers plus face bubbles; Coulomb friction and stick/slip updates.

Pressure-temperature solve

Darcy flow and Fourier heat conduction assembled in the pressure-temperature block, with thermodynamic/material functions separated in law files.

Coupling, diagnostics, visualization

Porosity and aperture updates, fixed-stress style relaxation, residual/iteration diagnostics, and ParaView/Ensight output generation.

What the code demonstrates

mechanics

VEM k=1 elasticity

Cell gradients, projection operators, polytopal geometry support, and fracture-interface displacement jumps.

contact

Nitsche or mixed LM contact

Switchable contact formulations, constant multipliers per fracture face, optional bubble DOFs, and normal stiffness control.

friction

Coulomb stick/slip logic

Friction angle, tangential jumps, active-set style updates, and contact residual/Jacobian routines.

flow and heat

Coupled P–T system

Pressure and temperature unknowns, Darcy/Fourier fluxes, thermodynamic functions, and fracture aperture effects.

coupling

Fixed-stress style relaxation

Porosity and aperture updates used to stabilise poromechanical coupling between matrix and fractures.

linear algebra

Sparse direct and iterative assets

SuperLU-backed sparse solves, BLAS/LAPACK support, and GMRES-related routines present in the repository for solver experimentation.
